# Code of conduct

Treat participants with respect. Discuss behavior, evidence and tradeoffs without personal attacks. Make room for questions, corrections and different experiences. Respect the privacy of contributors and people mentioned in issues, logs or test material.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse and publishing another person's private information are unacceptable in project spaces. Maintainers may remove offending content, close discussions or restrict participation to protect the community.

For a conduct concern, ask for a private contact route through the project [support channels](SUPPORT.md) and share sensitive details only through that route. Use the [security policy](SECURITY.md) for suspected software vulnerabilities.
